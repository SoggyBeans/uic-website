Statement of faith

United by a common faith.

Unity in Christ is UC Berkeley’s Christian inter-fellowship group. We promote unity among Christians on campus and support one another in prayer, service, evangelism, and fellowship.

“I have given them the glory that you gave me, that they may be one as we are one: I in them and you in me, so that they may become completely one.”

John 17:22–23

We affirm our common faith in:

  1. One God—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—existing eternally in holy love.
  2. God’s sovereignty in creation, revelation, redemption, and final judgment.
  3. The divine inspiration, truthfulness, and authority of Holy Scripture.
  4. The universal reality of sin and the need for God’s grace.
  5. Jesus Christ: fully God and fully human; his sinless life, saving death, bodily resurrection, and reign.
  6. Forgiveness and reconciliation with God through Jesus Christ alone.
  7. Salvation by God’s undeserved mercy, received through faith.
  8. The Holy Spirit’s renewing presence and work in believers.
  9. The universal Church as Christ’s body, made up of all true believers.
  10. The future return of Jesus Christ and his perfect judgment.
